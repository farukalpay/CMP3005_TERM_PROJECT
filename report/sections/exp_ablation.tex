We therefore re-ran the GA with a purely random initial population (same seeds and budgets). Without the seed the GA is better on 1 of 6 instances; A-n32-k5: 0.70\,\% instead of 5.48\,\% (8 runs at the BKS); A-n45-k6: 3.59\,\% instead of 1.15\,\%; A-n60-k9: 3.29\,\% instead of 3.12\,\%; A-n80-k10: 4.04\,\% instead of 3.12\,\%; MediDrone-7273: 3.70\,\% instead of 3.03\,\%; MediDrone-IST-7273: 3.95\,\% instead of 1.59\,\%. Across the six instances the unseeded runs end in 52 distinct final costs versus 34 for the seeded ones: a single strong seed buys a good start at the price of diversity --- exactly the trade-off that diversity-aware survival in hybrid genetic search is designed to manage \citep{vidal2012hybrid}.
