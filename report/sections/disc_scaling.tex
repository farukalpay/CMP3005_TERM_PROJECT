\emph{Size changes the ranking.} Up to $n\approx200$ TS and MA are on par, but from $n=250$ to $1000$ MA is better on every X instance (mean gap 1.12\,\% vs.\ 2.04\,\% for $n>300$): best-improvement TS pays $\Theta(nk)$ evaluations per applied move (\cref{sec:complexity}), which is affordable for 100 clinics and not for 1000 within one minute.
