\section{Parameter sensitivity and the Bosphorus effect}
\subsection*{Tabu tenure}
Tenure controls how long removed edges remain forbidden. Very short tenure allows quick
reversals and cycling. Very long tenure can block useful moves. The stored study varies tenure
on the team instance, fixing the candidate list at 20, with five seeds and ten seconds per run.

\begin{figure}[h]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{\figdir/sensitivity.pdf}
\caption{Left: tenure ranges with tuning seeds 101--105. Right: crossing routes in the best
saved team-instance plan at each penalty. Both plots reuse existing evidence.}
\end{figure}

Middle ranges perform better than very small or large ranges. The final range 10--25 lies in
this favourable region. This supports a moderate tenure for this instance, rather than a
universally optimal setting. Final evaluation uses different seeds, 1--10.

\subsection*{What changing the crossing penalty actually did}
The saved sweep covers $P=0$ to 1000. The best synthetic plans retain ten crossing routes
throughout; the cached analysis also records unchanged route structure. Increasing $P$ therefore
does not reorganize this instance's best saved routes.

European clinics are grouped far from the depot, and capacity already requires at least ten
drones to serve them. A plan reaching that minimum with one out-and-back crossing per such
drone cannot reduce crossing cost simply by increasing $P$. For that unchanged plan, increasing
$P$ by one adds 20 to total cost.

The optional real-geography extension does show route changes, but it is a different instance.
Its result cannot be presented as a route change on the required synthetic instance.

\subsection*{Why pure GA trails TS here}
TS explicitly relocates clinics between routes and improves visit order. Pure GA modifies a
giant-tour permutation and relies on Split to create routes. Some useful assignment changes
can be harder to obtain through those permutation operators. The saved optional local-search
GA performs much better, which is consistent with the value of route-level improvement.
This is an interpretation of the observed behaviour, not a proof that the GA representation
is inherently inferior.
