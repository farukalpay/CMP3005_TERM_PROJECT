\section{Search progress and the delivery plan}
\begin{figure}[h]\centering
\includegraphics[width=.94\linewidth]{\figdir/convergence.pdf}
\caption{Best cost so far on A-n45-k6. Lines are medians; shading shows the middle 50\% of ten
runs. Time is logarithmic. Curves start only when all ten runs have a feasible answer.}
\end{figure}

TS finds a strong solution early here. GA improves more gradually and finishes with different
costs across seeds. Best-so-far cost never increases: the current state may worsen, but the
algorithm retains its best feasible answer. Early success does not end a run here; the methods
continue for the full common time budget.

\begin{figure}[h]\centering
\includegraphics[width=.86\linewidth]{\figdir/routes.pdf}
\caption{A best saved TS plan for MediDrone-7273, cost 20,724.21095. Each colour is a route.
The star is the depot and the dashed line is the synthetic Bosphorus boundary.}
\end{figure}

This plan uses 17 of the available 18 drones. Ten routes visit Europe; each crosses twice,
giving 20 crossing edges. Their penalty is $20\times150=3000$ cost units.
European demand is 938, requiring at least $\lceil938/100\rceil=10$ drones. The plan reaches
that simple crossing-route lower bound. This certifies the crossing count, not minimum total distance.
